03 · Regulatory: compliance is the moat

FCC (2025): AI-generated voices = "artificial or prerecorded voice" under TCPA → prior express written consent required for outbound AI calls. Florida (FTSA), Oklahoma, California add state penalties + private right of action.

The safe model is inbound-first: AI captures form fills, follows up instantly, confirms appointments, cuts no-shows. We only contact homeowners who asked to be contacted.

  • Speed-to-lead: >50% of jobs go to the contractor who answers first — instant AI follow-up wins deals
  • No-shows: 15–25% baseline without confirmation → AI confirm + reminders recover dispatch hours
  • Marketing angle: "FCC-compliant AI — we never cold-call homeowners illegally." Competitors who cold-call are one fine away from being a cautionary tale.
